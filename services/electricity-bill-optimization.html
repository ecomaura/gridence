Questions on this service

What is electricity bill optimisation?

It is a review of your electricity bills, tariff category and supply arrangements to identify billing discrepancies and opportunities to optimise expenditure. Results depend on your site.

What is sanctioned load?

Sanctioned load is the maximum electrical load your connection is approved for by the utility. If it does not match actual needs, you may be paying for more than you use, or risk exceeding limits.

What is contract demand?

Contract demand is the demand level agreed with the utility for certain commercial and industrial connections. It influences demand-related charges on your bill.

What is Open Access electricity?

Open Access allows eligible large consumers to source electricity from suppliers other than their local distribution company, subject to regulations.
