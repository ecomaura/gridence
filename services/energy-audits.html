Energy Audits & Electricity Safety Audits

Energy audits and electricity safety audits give a business a clear technical picture of how electricity is used and where the installation may carry risk. They support decisions on efficiency, safety and regulatory readiness for hotels, restaurants and other commercial and industrial operations.

What the service covers

Energy audits

Review how electrical energy is consumed across your premises and identify inefficiencies that increase waste.

Electricity safety audits

Inspect the installation for electrical safety hazards and gaps against applicable regulatory requirements.

Reducing energy waste

Translate findings into practical recommendations that improve operational efficiency.

Regulatory requirements

Understand which electrical safety obligations apply to your type of establishment.

Business benefits

  • Clearer visibility of how your facility uses electricity
  • Early identification of electrical safety hazards
  • Recommendations to reduce energy waste
  • Better regulatory readiness for hotels, restaurants and other businesses

Questions on this service

Why do I need an energy audit?

An energy audit shows where electricity is being wasted and what can be improved in efficiency and operations.

What is an electrical safety audit?

It is an inspection of your electrical installation for hazards and gaps against regulatory requirements and safety standards.
