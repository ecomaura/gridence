Regulatory Compliance & Closure Services

Electrical faults are a recognised cause of fire and operational risk. GRIDENCE assesses your installation against safety standards and regulatory requirements, then helps close the gaps so legal and operational exposure is reduced for hotels, restaurants, hospitals and industries.

What the service covers

Fire hazards

Identify electrical conditions that can contribute to fire risk.

Compliance assessments

Assess your installation against applicable regulatory requirements.

Risk identification

Document legal and operational risks in plain terms.

Closure of compliance gaps

Coordinate the work and paperwork needed to close identified gaps.

Safety standards

Align electrical practices to accepted safety standards.
